Apple Event Live Blog: iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro, and More Expected
Last Updated: Wednesday September 9, 2026 11:32 am PDT
 by 
Jordan Golson
Apple's "Surprise and Shine" event kicks off today at 10:00 a.m. Pacific Time, where we're expecting to see the foldable iPhone Duo, the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 models, new AirPods 5 earphones, and perhaps some other announcements.
Apple is providing a 
live video stream
 on its website, on YouTube, and in the company's TV app across various platforms. We will also be updating this article with live blog coverage and issuing Twitter updates through our 
@MacRumorsLive
 account as the event unfolds. Highlights from the event and separate news stories regarding today's announcements will go out through our 
@MacRumors
 account.
As usual, Apple's 
online store is down for updates
 ahead of the event.
Sign up
 for our newsletter to keep up with Apple news and rumors.
Live Updates - No need to refresh
10:35 am
There's a second model with "some great additional features" starting with on-stem volume control.
10:34 am
AirPods 5 are $129, same price as the previous generation without ANC.
10:34 am
They're "reengineered to be more durable than ever" and more than 4 hours of listening with ANC and 20 hours total with the charging case.
10:34 am
"Together with iPhone, AirPods enable you to leverage the power of intelligence while staying present with those around you."
10:34 am
AirPods 5 has hands-free access to Siri with Hey Siri, as well as Live Translation.
10:33 am
"AirPods 5 are a seamless extension of Apple Intelligence on your iPhone."
10:32 am
"The industry's best ANC in an open-ear design."
10:32 am
"AirPods 5 feature our beloved open-air fit." AirPods 5 deliver a richer, more detailed sound across a wider range of ear geometries. New active noise cancellation, with 50% more noise reduction than AirPods 4.
10:31 am
"This is AirPods 5."
10:31 am
Interestingly, we're moving from the new iPhone Pro to AirPods — normally, headphones would come first.
10:30 am
Adds AppleCare One Family option. Pre-order September 12, available September 18.
10:30 am
Starts at $1199 with 256GB, Pro Max starts at $1299. There is a 2TB option now. Apple Upgrade starts at $34.99/month for 24 months (or lower with trade-in). Discounts of up to $1200 off with trade in of iPhone 14 or newer.
10:29 am
New accessories including an upgraded clear case, and new colors for the existing Apple iPhone cases and wrist straps.
10:29 am
iPhone Handoff launches with T-Mobile and Telecom Germany. Verizon and EE launch by the end of the year, with more carriers to follow.
10:28 am
They're giving a brief overview of what's coming in iOS 27, most of which we've seen before.
10:28 am
"There's an upgraded dynamic island... it can now show three live activities."
10:27 am
The whole event was filmed on iPhone 18 Pro.
10:26 am
"Let's talk about image authenticity..." New feature called Apple Reference Image, proving the authenticity of a photo taken on Apple 18 Pro. The main camera signs photo data at a pixel level at the time of capture. Private cloud compute turns the signed data into an unalterable image that lives in the Photos app. "It's like having a digital negative."
10:25 am
Improvements to photographic styles, including adjustments to skin texture.
10:25 am
Pro controls work for both photos and video. "Making iPhone 18 Pro ready for everything from professional film sets to the things you should every day." Smart focus tracking uses on-device AI models to lock onto a subject and maintain detail, even if the subject leaves the screen and then comes back.
10:24 am
New camera feature called "Pro Controls", allowing you to change white balance or view a histogram, manually adjust the aperture and change shutter speed "for truly unique shots that weren't possible before on iPhone."
10:23 am
Time-lapse now supports 4K resolution in Dolby Vision HDR. "You can now get stunning cinematic mode effects from regular video after you capture, and it now supports 60 frames per second for this half-speed effect." And the new Audio Mix feature now supports music.
10:22 am
"The aperture adjusts automatically based on the scene. When you're in a darker environment, the aperture opens to let in around 50% more light, like in this portrait with impressive detail and color range."
This helps explains the opening showing all the impressive dynamic range.
10:22 am
"To create variable aperture, we developed a system of six laser-cut blades, each thinner than a human hair. The blades are made of a specialized polymer composite for the right balance of mass flexibility and durability."
10:21 am
"iPhone 18 Pro has a new 48 megapixel fusion main camera with variable aperture for creative control that wasn't possible before. This is the most advanced camera we've ever made."
10:21 am
"One of the fundamental pillars of photography is aperture. It controls how much light enters the system and affects every photo and video you take. Being able to change the aperture provides more control for light intake and depth of field."
10:21 am
They're showing an impressive gaming demo and then quickly moving on to the camera.
10:20 am
iPhone 18 Pro takes another massive leap enabled by A20 Pro silicon efficiencies, despite the 18 Pro's smaller size, it delivers the same battery life as iPhone 18 Pro Max. iPhone 18 Pro Max has the largest increase in battery life ever on an iPhone. 36 hours of video playback on iPhone 18 Pro, with 45 hours of video playback on the iPhone 18 Pro Max. For typical use, 18 Pro gets up to 24 hours per charge and 30 hours per charge on Pro Max. Wired charging is faster, and iPhone can charge 50% in around 15 minutes, and 5 minutes of wired charging provides 6 hours of video playback.
10:18 am
"These are giant leaps in sustained performance."
10:18 am
"iPhone 18 Pro has a next-generation vapor chamber that helps the system manage even more heat. It has three times the surface area as the one in iPhone 17 Pro. Deionized water is constantly cycled throughout the system for significantly improved boroughs."
10:17 am
"Effective thermal management is critical because efficient heat dissipation is what enables the maximum performance from this incredible chip."
10:17 am
The silicon packaging has been redesigned, with "custom packaging inspired by our M-series chips" connecting silicon and the memory directly to the vapor chamber for improved thermal performance.
10:16 am
New GPU has 7-core design with up to 40% faster graphics performance, with increased bandwidth and new neural accelerators with 2x faster 8FP math. Helps with running 3rd-party LLMs on device. The Neural Engine has been advancing year after year, with a second neural engine being added for a total of 32 cores. " A20 Pro is the ultimate chip for running advanced on-device models... this is the widest memory interface we've ever shipped in an iPhone."
10:15 am
6-core CPU, 2 new super cores that are up to 20% faster, and 4 new efficiency cores. The super cores are "desktop class" and "the fastest in any smartphone."
10:15 am
The A20 Pro is "an exceptional chip that sets a new bar for mobile computing. A brand new architecture built on 2nm technology."
10:14 am
Now we're going deeper into the Apple Silicon in the new iPhone 18 Pro.
10:14 am
"Some features will have usage limits because they rely on powerful server models. Increased access will be available with most iCloud Plus plans."
10:12 am
The new model improves on-device dictation as well.
10:12 am
"Siri AI sounds incredible. That's because iPhone 18 Pro takes advantage of our most advanced on-device model ever, which powers expressive voices. You can pick from multiple options, then customize the pace and expressivity."
10:11 am
"Siri is built right into the camera app on your iPhone, so you can ask questions and take actions, like adding multiple events to your calendar all in one go."
10:11 am
"Have rich conversations with Siri by using voice or typing across your iPhone, or in the all-new Siri app that's right on your home screen."
10:10 am
"Siri can take actions in your apps, including third-party apps."
10:10 am
Quickly moving on to the new version of Siri. "Siri finds your personal context across apps, like Mail and Messages."
10:09 am
Apple is starting off with Apple Intelligence, pushing Apple's on-device models and Private Cloud Compute.
10:09 am
Comes in a deep black, a refined silver, a new "Glacier" blue, and burgandy, a "sophisticated and unique new color."
10:08 am
It starts with the A20 Pro, with "the longest battery life in iPhone history." "This is what happens when you go to the moon, come back and ask what's next." 
It's the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. We're starting with the Pro, not the regular iPhone.
10:07 am
"We're going to take these experiences even further with the next generation of iPhone." Starting with a shot of the moon, taken on the iPhone 17 Pro.
10:07 am
"Not even Apple can access what's yours." The Ternus era is starting with an aggressive push on privacy versus what other companies are offering.
10:06 am
"It understands your context... the most private details of your daily life. Others see that data as something to collect and store. They move it to their servers and ask you to trust them... but trust only goes so far when your data is no longer yours to control."
10:05 am
"You'd need a large, beautiful display to show rich content. You'd add high-quality cameras and microphones, so it could see and hear and reason over the sights, sounds, and the world around you. Of course, you give it all-day battery life. You'd want it to be super easy to use without a big learning curve, and finally, you'd want it to work seamlessly with your other devices, apps, and services, so it feels like one integrated experience. experience. In other words, you would arrive at something remarkably familiar, because there's no product in the world better designed to be your intelligent personal hub than iPhone."
10:05 am
"Now, if you were designing the ideal version of this hub from scratch, how would you do it? Well, first, you would want something that is always with you, deeply personal, and able to bring intelligence to the moments when it matters most. You'd require require a powerful processor that can run incredibly capable on-device models to quickly answer questions in the moment and pervasive networking to access knowledge and even more capable models in the cloud."
10:04 am
"And this means the product at the center of your experiences becomes even more essential. What I like to think of as an intelligent personal hub. It's the intersection of broad new capabilities. and a deep understanding of your personal context that makes this idea so powerful."
10:04 am
"The values at the heart of Apple continue to guide everything we do. As we look ahead, we see a future filled with enormous discovery and transformation. AI makes entirely new kinds of experiences possible, and AI can become even more useful when it brings together your life with the apps, services, and products you use every day."
10:03 am
"Our teams bring an incredible level of collaboration and care to everythign they do, all in service of a mission that has guided us from the very beginning: to make the best products in the world."
10:03 am
Now we're flying over Big Sur, following a car to Apple Park... "before we reveal our main character... Not me, that's your guy." Tim Cook appears one last time, handing off to new CEO John Ternus.
10:02 am
As Apple has been known to do recently, it is showing just how great the iPhone can look when shooting video, albeit it seems likely that all manner of high-production value add ons are being used.
10:01 am
It starts with an extremely dark "movie" being shot with fire and explosions, showing some rather extreme dynamic range.
10:00 am
"The following was shot on iPhone"
10:00 am
As the clock strikes 10 a.m. PDT, the music changes and the presentation begins!
9:49 am
The stream will also be on YouTube, but that typically goes live at the last moment around 10 a.m. PDT.
9:49 am
The live stream is up on Apple's home page, currently displaying shifting colors and shapes in the theme of the event invite with a chill soundtrack.
9:39 am
We're in the room!
8:43 am
As usual, Apple is providing light breakfast items for everyone.
8:36 am
We're on site at Apple Park, where attendees are checking in to get their badges and milling around ahead of the event. Under 90 minutes to go!
